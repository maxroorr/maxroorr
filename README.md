## About Masroor Shahani

**Masroor Shahani** is the **Founder & CEO of NEXORA LLC**, a software and digital agency that designs, builds and launches **websites, e-commerce stores, Android apps, SaaS platforms, business dashboards and data automation** for clients. With a background in data operations, he leads every project from the first idea to launch and growth.

He is also the founder of **The Ray Perfumes**, and shares business and tech knowledge through **Dhanda Dimaag** and **Masroor Digital Desk**.

- 🚀 Leads product strategy, design and delivery at NEXORA LLC
- 🧩 Builds member portals, admin panels and multi-role platforms
- 📊 Turns raw business data into dashboards, reports and automated workflows
- 📱 Ships Android apps with automated build and release pipelines
- 🖥️ Publishes Windows desktop apps to the Microsoft Store (Sendvora, SALVAGE)
- 📈 Grows brands with SEO, content and social media marketing
